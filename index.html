Our story

Grown on the Prairies.

Fear the Sheaf is a 100% Saskatchewan owned & operated apparel company, started right here in Martensville. We exist to back the athletes who put in the work, and the elite sport that makes this province proud.

Every piece is made to be worn hard: at the rink, on the field, in the gym, and everywhere in between. Quality first. Saskatchewan always.

All store policies
When will my pre-order be ready?
Pre-orders lock in your size from the first run. We'll text or email you as soon as your gear is ready, and you can cancel for a full refund any time before then. Pre-order details
How does pickup work?
Pick Pickup at checkout. It's free. Once your order is ready, we'll reach out to set up a time in Martensville.
Do you deliver or ship?
Yes. Saskatoon-area delivery is $5 and shipping anywhere in Canada is $15. Shipped orders come with tracking. Shipping details
How do the sizes run?
Check the for chest and length measurements. Between sizes? Size up for a more relaxed fit.
What if it doesn't fit?
No stress. Unworn items with tags can be swapped for a different size. Returns & exchanges
Do you have promo codes?
Watch our Instagram for drops and codes. If you have one, enter it on the secure checkout page under your order summary.
Is checkout secure?
Yes. Payments run through Stripe, so your card details never touch our site. We take Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
Do you do custom or team gear?
We're all about custom SK apparel. Send us a message on Instagram and tell us what you have in mind.
