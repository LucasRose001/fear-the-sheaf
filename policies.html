Shipping & Pickup

Free pickup in Martensville

Choose Pickup at checkout and we'll text or email you when your order is ready, along with pickup details. In-stock items are usually ready within 2-3 business days.

Saskatoon-area delivery ($5)

We deliver to Saskatoon, Martensville, Warman and nearby communities. We'll reach out to arrange a time.

Shipping within Canada ($15)

Orders ship within 3-5 business days and typically arrive in 3-8 business days after that, depending on where you are. We'll send tracking once it's on its way. We currently ship to Canadian addresses only.

Lost or damaged packages

If your order shows up damaged or doesn't arrive, contact us and we'll make it right.

Returns & Exchanges

Wrong size? No problem.

We're happy to exchange for a different size within 14 days of receiving your order, as long as it's unworn, unwashed and has its tags.

Returns

Unworn items can be returned within 14 days for a refund to your original payment method. Shipping fees aren't refundable, and for shipped orders the return shipping is paid by the customer.

How to start one

Contact us with your name and order details. Local customers can drop off in Martensville; we'll send instructions for shipped returns. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of us receiving the item, and may take a few more days to show up on your statement.

Defects

If something arrives with a flaw, let us know within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you, no charge.

Payments

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. All prices are in Canadian dollars. Your card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store them.

Privacy

What we collect

When you order, we receive your name, email, phone number and (for delivery or shipping) your address. We use this only to fill your order and contact you about it.

What we don't do

We never sell or share your information with anyone for marketing. Payment info is processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy.

Your cart

This site remembers what's in your cart using your browser's local storage, so it's there when you come back. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us until you check out.

Your choices

Want us to update or delete your info? Just contact us.
